Meet Ustazah Malik Urooj Humza

Assalamu Alaikum Wa Rahmatullahi Wa Barakatuh. I am Malik Urooj Humza, an educator dedicated to bridging structured analytical teaching methods with the timeless beauty of classical Islamic scholarship.

Having completed my Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) and currently advancing through my Master of Arts (M.A.) in Islamic Studies, I bring both modern pedagogical organization and authentic scriptural depth to every lesson.

Many parents and sisters struggle to find a reliable female teacher who explains Tajweed rules clearly in English and Urdu while nurturing a genuine love for Deen. My online academy was founded to offer a dignified, calm, and structured learning space where every student progresses at their own pace.

Structured Learning Pathways

Sacred Curriculum & Courses

Every course is tailored to the student's age, current proficiency, and learning goals, taught live in one-on-one sessions by Ustazah Malik Urooj Humza.

Level 01 • Foundation القاعدة النورانية

Noorani Qaida & Foundational Makharij

Designed for young children (ages 4+) and absolute beginners. Builds accurate Arabic letter recognition, phonetic articulation points, vowel combinations (Harakat), and letter joining rules.

  • ✓ Correct Arabic pronunciation from day one
  • ✓ Interactive visual drills & patient repetition
  • ✓ Smooth transition to reading the Mushaf
Level 02 • Recitation ناظرة القرآن بالتجويد

Nazra Quran with Applied Tajweed Rules

Complete fluent reading of the Holy Quran with practical application of Tajweed principles, including Noon & Meem Sakinah, Qalqalah, Madd rules, and proper stopping (Waqf).

  • ✓ Elimination of hidden and obvious recitation errors
  • ✓ Color-coded Tajweed Mushaf screen sharing
  • ✓ Weekly revision & fluency evaluation
Level 03 • Memorization تحفيظ القرآن الكريم

Hifz al-Quran (Juz Amma & Selected Surahs)

Structured, low-stress memorization program for children and sisters. Covers Juz Amma (30th Para), Surah Al-Mulk, Surah Yaseen, Surah Al-Kahf, and Surah Ar-Rahman.

  • ✓ 3-tier system: Sabaq (New), Sabaqi, and Manzil
  • ✓ Retention-first methodology so verses are never forgotten
  • ✓ Ideal for school-going kids & busy adults
Level 04 • Tarbiyah الدراسات الإسلامية

Essential Islamic Studies, Salah & Duas

Holistic Islamic upbringing for children and teens living in Western or local communities. Teaches daily Masnoon Duas, Six Kalimas, complete Salah method, and prophetic manners (Akhlaq).

  • ✓ Practical Wudu & Namaz training with translation
  • ✓ Stories of the Prophets & Seerah lessons
  • ✓ Included complimentary alongside Quran classes

How Our Online Classroom Works

Combining warm Islamic etiquette (Adab) with modern interactive tools so every student stays attentive and inspired.

I

1-on-1 Individual Classes

Your child or sister is never placed in a crowded group call. Every 30-minute session is 100% private and focused solely on one student.

II

Screen-Shared Digital Mushaf

Interactive whiteboard annotations and color-coded Tajweed scripts on Zoom or Google Meet allow students to see every rule highlighted live.

III

Safe Environment for Sisters

Taught directly by Ustazah Malik Urooj Humza, ensuring complete privacy, comfort, and modesty for female students and young children.

IV

Monthly Parent Feedback

Parents receive regular WhatsApp updates and monthly progress assessments detailing Tajweed accuracy, Surahs memorized, and Duas learned.
